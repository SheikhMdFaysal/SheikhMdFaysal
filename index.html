Who I am

About Me

I'm a researcher and analytics professional working at the intersection of AI governance, explainability, and financial systems. My work proposes structured frameworks for making AI accountable in high-stakes domains — from financial services to ESG scoring — grounded in rigorous bibliometric methodology (PRISMA, VOSviewer).

As lead researcher, I've conducted bibliometric reviews of 405 peer-reviewed articles proposing TRACE (Accountable AI in Finance) and 3,235 papers proposing GLASS (Explainability in AI-Driven ESG Scoring), both presented at the 2026 Montclair State University Student Research Symposium with manuscripts in progress.

I hold an MS in Business Analytics (STEM) from Montclair State University (May 2026, GPA: 3.83) and bring nine years of applied experience in financial planning & analysis, net revenue analytics, and decision-support systems at a $2B+ regulated utility — experience that directly informs how I think about governance and accountability in deployed AI systems.
